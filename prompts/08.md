## Implementation Notes

`LocalDevKekProvider` exists only for local development and tests. Production deployments must use a recoverable KEK backed by KMS, TPM, Nitro Enclave, SGX sealing or recipient public-key wrapping.

The in-memory `NonceRegistry` is suitable only for local tests. Production deployments require durable or event-sourced nonce/replay tracking.
